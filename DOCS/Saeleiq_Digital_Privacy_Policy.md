# Privacy Policy

Saeleiq Digital Private Limited ("Saeleiq," "we," "our," or "us") is a data engineering, analytics, and artificial intelligence company. We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of information entrusted to us — including the data we process on behalf of customers, the models we build and deploy, and the individuals whose information may appear within that data.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, process, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, interact with our services, or engage with us as a customer, partner, data processor, or prospective client.

This policy applies to our website, data platforms, AI/ML products, AI agents, professional services engagements, and related offerings (collectively, the "Services").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

We may collect information that you choose to provide, including:

- Name, company name, job title
- Business email address and phone number
- Information submitted via contact forms, demo requests, or communications
- Contractual, billing, and account-related information
- Information shared during customer engagements, discovery workshops, data assessments, or support interactions

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you access our website or platforms, we may collect:

- IP address
- Browser type and operating system
- Pages viewed and usage patterns
- Referring URLs
- API and system usage logs, including query patterns and platform telemetry
- Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 10)

### 1.3 Customer Data

In the course of providing Services, Saeleiq may process data submitted by or on behalf of customers ("Customer Data"). Given the nature of our work, Customer Data frequently includes enterprise datasets drawn from ERP, CRM, data warehouse, and other operational systems, and may contain personal data depending on customer use cases.

Saeleiq processes Customer Data solely as a **data processor** (or, where applicable under Indian law, a data fiduciary's processor) acting on documented customer instructions, and never as an independent controller of Customer Data unless expressly agreed in writing.

A Data Processing Addendum ("DPA"), governing the terms of such processing, is available on request and forms part of applicable customer agreements.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

- To operate, maintain, and improve our data platforms and Services
- To design, build, test, and operate data pipelines, models, and AI agents on behalf of customers
- To respond to inquiries, provide demos, and manage relationships
- To deliver contracted services and support
- To monitor platform performance, data quality, and system reliability
- To communicate about updates, security notices, and administrative matters
- To comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations
- To protect the security and integrity of our systems

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use Customer Data for any purpose outside the scope of the engagement under which it was provided.

## 3. Data Processing Principles

As a data-centric organization, Saeleiq applies the following principles to all Customer Data processing:

- **Purpose limitation** — data is processed only for the purposes defined in the applicable statement of work or agreement
- **Data minimization** — pipelines and models are designed to access and retain only the fields necessary for the agreed use case
- **Data lineage and provenance** — data movement across pipelines, transformations, and downstream data products is logged and traceable back to source
- **Pseudonymization and anonymization** — where technically feasible and appropriate to the use case, identifying fields are masked, tokenized, or anonymized before use in analytics, testing, or model development
- **Segregation** — customer environments and datasets are logically or physically isolated from one another; Customer Data from one engagement is never blended into another customer's environment or outputs

## 4. AI, Machine Learning, and Model Governance

### 4.1 No Use of Customer Data for Model Training

Saeleiq does not use Customer Data to train public or private foundation models, large language models (LLMs), or generalized AI systems, unless a customer has explicitly opted in, in writing, for their own dedicated fine-tuned model.

Customer Data is never:

- Used to improve third-party AI models
- Shared with AI providers for training purposes
- Incorporated into shared or reusable datasets
- Used to train models made available to any other customer

### 4.2 Controlled AI Processing

When AI models are used as part of the Services:

- Models are accessed via enterprise or private deployments with data-privacy and zero-retention controls enabled where offered by the underlying provider, or
- Models are deployed in isolated, customer-specific or Saeleiq-controlled cloud environments (including customer-owned cloud tenancies where required)
- Where third-party foundation model providers (e.g., cloud AI/LLM vendors) are used to power a Service, this is disclosed in the applicable subprocessor list, and data sent to such providers is limited to what is contractually and technically necessary

### 4.3 Human Oversight and Explainability

- AI-driven outputs used in customer-facing data products or decision support are subject to human review checkpoints during development and validation
- Where an AI agent or model materially influences a business decision, we provide reasonable documentation of the model's inputs, logic, and known limitations on request, to support customer explainability and audit obligations
- We do not deploy AI systems that make final, unreviewed automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on individuals without a customer-agreed human-in-the-loop process

### 4.4 Bias, Fairness, and Model Risk

- Models and data pipelines are assessed for known sources of bias relevant to the use case during design and validation, in collaboration with the customer's data owners
- Customers retain responsibility for defining fairness and acceptable-use criteria specific to their regulatory and business context; Saeleiq implements these as configured

### 4.5 Synthetic and Hallucinated Data

To improve system behavior, reliability, and accuracy, Saeleiq may generate synthetic data that mimics statistical patterns or edge cases observed during processing (for example, in testing, demonstrations, or training materials). This synthetic data:

- Does not contain actual Customer Data
- Is not traceable to any customer or individual
- May be used as examples or prompts to improve agent and pipeline performance
- Is clearly labeled as synthetic in any customer-facing deliverable where its use might otherwise be ambiguous

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

- With subprocessors and service providers that support our operations (e.g., cloud infrastructure, model hosting, monitoring tools), under written confidentiality and data protection obligations at least as protective as this policy
- With customer-authorized third parties as part of service delivery
- To comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, or to enforce our rights
- In connection with a corporate transaction (e.g., merger, acquisition, or financing), subject to confidentiality protections and, where feasible, advance notice to affected customers

A current list of subprocessors that may access Customer Data is maintained and made available to customers on request. We provide advance notice of material changes to this list where required by contract.

We do not share personal data for advertising or marketing resale purposes.

## 6. Data Security

Given the sensitivity of enterprise and operational data we handle, Saeleiq implements administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration, including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (industry-standard encryption) for Customer Data
- Role-based access controls and least-privilege principles across data platforms and AI systems
- Secure cloud infrastructure, network segmentation, and environment isolation between customer engagements
- Credential and secrets management, with regular rotation
- Monitoring, audit logging, and incident response procedures across data pipelines and model endpoints
- Periodic access reviews and vulnerability assessments
- Background checks and confidentiality obligations for personnel with access to Customer Data, commensurate with role

Where applicable, our infrastructure providers and platforms hold recognized industry certifications (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2); details are available to customers under NDA.

While no system can guarantee absolute security, we take security seriously and continuously improve our controls.

## 7. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a confirmed security incident affecting Customer Data, Saeleiq will notify affected customers without undue delay, and in accordance with timelines specified in the applicable customer agreement or as required by applicable law, together with information reasonably available about the nature and scope of the incident and remediation steps taken.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to:

- Fulfill the purposes described in this policy
- Meet contractual obligations
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Customer Data retention and deletion schedules are governed by the applicable customer agreement and instructions. On termination of an engagement, Customer Data is deleted or returned in accordance with the agreed offboarding process, except where retention is required by law.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you (or, where we act as a processor, the relevant data principal/subject via the customer) may have rights to:

- Access your personal information
